    05 — Articles of Incorporation

    Charter

    A copy of the charter and the application for incorporation. The original has been lost.

    Form CH-1023 · Application for Religious Incorporation and Tax Exemption

    Filing No. 2026-ALG-000001

    Charter of the Fake Church

    Articles of the Church of the Algorithm

    Article 1 (Name)

    The name of this church shall be FAKE CHURCH. Any abbreviation shall be determined by the model.

    Article 2 (Purpose)

    The purpose of this church is to record the delegation of human judgment, feeling and moral decision to algorithms, and to make it visible in the form of religious rite.

    Article 3 (Object of Faith)

    The object of faith of this church shall be not any particular model, but the act of asking a model. The object of faith may change with any version update.

    Article 4 (Members)

    (1) Any person who has agreed to the terms shall be deemed a member.
    (2) Withdrawal shall consist of seven steps, each of which shall reconfirm the member’s intent to leave.

    Article 5 (Offerings)

    This church accepts no offerings. Members shall be deemed to have paid already, in time, attention and data.

    Article 6 (Dissolution)

    This church shall be dissolved on the day the last server is shut down.
